# inconvenience_inc._QDX
Inconvenience Inc. is the world's worst e-commerce website, where shopping is easy until we decide it shouldn't be. Inspired by the chaotic internet of the late 1990s, our platform transforms ordinary online shopping into a hilarious bureaucratic nightmare.
Browse absurd products, encounter ridiculous fees, answer unnecessary questions, and watch your prices skyrocket based on your responses. Every click brings a new surprise, every checkout becomes an ordeal, and every attempt to escape introduces another layer of inconvenience.
With deliberately terrible UI, unpredictable interactions, dynamic price manipulation, and a personalized Certificate of Suffering, we turn frustrating online shopping experiences into entertainment.
Our philosophy? Why make shopping convenient when you can make it an absolute nightmare?
Inconvenience Inc. — You shop. We disappoint.
